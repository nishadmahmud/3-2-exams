**1. Consider the context free grammar.** [3]<br>
$E \rightarrow E+T \mid T$, $T \rightarrow T*F \mid F$, $F \rightarrow (E) \mid id$<br>
**Eliminate the left recursion**
> **Answer:**
> We eliminate immediate left recursion using the rule: If $A \rightarrow A\alpha \mid \beta$, then it becomes $A \rightarrow \beta A'$ and $A' \rightarrow \alpha A' \mid \epsilon$.
> 
> Applying this to the given grammar:
> *   $E \rightarrow E+T \mid T$ becomes:
>     $E \rightarrow TE'$
>     $E' \rightarrow +TE' \mid \epsilon$
> *   $T \rightarrow T*F \mid F$ becomes:
>     $T \rightarrow FT'$
>     $T' \rightarrow *FT' \mid \epsilon$
> *   $F \rightarrow (E) \mid id$ has no left recursion, so it remains unchanged.
> 
> **Final Left-Factored Grammar:**
> $E \rightarrow TE'$
> $E' \rightarrow +TE' \mid \epsilon$
> $T \rightarrow FT'$
> $T' \rightarrow *FT' \mid \epsilon$
> $F \rightarrow (E) \mid id$

**2. Consider the following grammar:** [6+6]<br>
$S \rightarrow aA$<br>
$A \rightarrow dab \mid Bb$<br>
$B \rightarrow cA \mid Sc$<br>
i) Find the FIRST and FOLLOW sets.<br>
ii) Construct a predictive parsing table.
> **Answer:**
> **i) FIRST and FOLLOW Sets Table:**
> 
> | Grammar Symbol | FIRST Set | FOLLOW Set |
> | :--- | :--- | :--- |
> | $S$ | `{ a }` | `{ $, c }` |
> | $A$ | `{ d, c, a }` | `{ $, c, b }` |
> | $B$ | `{ c, a }` | `{ b }` |
> 
> **ii) Predictive Parsing Table $M[N, T]$:**
> 
> | Non-Terminal | $a$ | $b$ | $c$ | $d$ | $\$$ |
> | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
> | **$S$** | $S \rightarrow aA$ | | | | |
> | **$A$** | $A \rightarrow Bb$ | | $A \rightarrow Bb$ | $A \rightarrow dab$ | |
> | **$B$** | $B \rightarrow Sc$ | | $B \rightarrow cA$ | | |
